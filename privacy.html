SiteSafety AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

SiteSafety AI & Job Site Suite ("the app") is a job-site safety and paperwork tool for construction foremen and their companies, published by Jason Briggs (developer name: Rezin92002). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

Whose data this is

The records in the app belong to the company using it — the foreman's own crew, job sites, and paperwork. Workers do not create accounts. A worker's name appears in the app only because their foreman's company runs a crew roster, the same information a paper sign-in sheet would hold.

Information the app collects and stores

Stored on the app's secure cloud server (hosted in the United States), so the company's records are backed up and the foreman's phone stays in step:

  • Crew roster entries — names, and phone numbers when the company enters them, with sign-in and sign-out times and finger-drawn signature images
  • Job-site records — daily shift codes, permit selections, time-off requests, and crew suggestions
  • Documents and logs — the safety documents, daily logs, and sealed records the foreman creates, including their contents
  • Site photos — photos the foreman takes or uploads for hazard analysis
  • Company settings — company and job-site details the foreman enters, including the office email address used for the send-to-office feature
  • Location — the foreman's device location, used at the moment it is requested to show weather for the job site. Location is not tracked in the background.
  • Questions and text sent to the AI features — safety questions, talk notes, and document details the foreman submits

The foreman's phone also keeps a working copy of the current records so the app works without a signal; that copy syncs to the server when a connection returns.

How information is used

Only to run the app: keeping the roster and sign-in record, producing the daily log and documents, analyzing site photos for hazards, writing and reading aloud the safety talk, answering safety questions, and showing site weather. Information is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared for marketing. The app contains no ads and no advertising trackers.

Service providers that process information for the app

  • Google Cloud & MongoDB Atlas — host the app's server and database
  • Google Gemini — receives photos, questions, and text the foreman submits to the AI features, and returns the analysis or answer
  • ElevenLabs — receives safety-talk text to produce the read-aloud audio
  • Open-Meteo — receives the job site's coordinates to return weather

Retention and deletion

Company records stay stored while the company uses the app. A foreman can delete documents and records inside the app. To have a company's stored data deleted from the server, or to ask what is stored, contact us at jasonbriggs8365@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Children

The app is a workplace tool for adults. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.
