One app for the whole shift

SiteSafety AI & Job Site Suite is built for the general foreman running the day: who's on site, what the hazards are, which permits are open, and what paperwork the office needs — without a clipboard, a binder, or a second trip back to the trailer.

Crew sign-in by shift code or QR

The crew signs in on their own phones from the crew page — no app install for workers. The roster builds itself as they arrive.

Safety talks, written for the day

The morning talk is generated for the day's real conditions and work, then read aloud to the crew — no generic script from a binder.

Photo hazard analysis

Snap a photo of the work area and get hazard findings with OSHA references, so the talk covers what's actually in front of the crew.

Permits tracked per day

16 permit types tracked against each day, so what's open, what's required, and what's done is always on the record.

46 OSHA documents

JHAs, inspections, and logs — 46 OSHA document types generated from the day's real data instead of typed up after hours.

Paperwork goes straight to the office

The daily log and timesheets are emailed straight to the office from the app. The day is documented before the foreman leaves the site.

Works offline

No signal on site? The day keeps running — sign-ins, talks, and paperwork continue, and everything syncs when the phone is back in range.

Time corrections, with a history

Wrong sign-in time? The foreman can correct sign-in times, and the edit history is kept — the record shows what changed and when.
